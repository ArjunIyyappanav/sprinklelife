Our Purification Process

From raw water to every finished bottle, each stage is carefully managed to maintain purity, quality and hygiene.

01

01 Raw Water Collection

Water is carefully collected and stored in a clean and sanitized storage tank before entering the purification process.

02

02 Sand Filtration

The first filtration stage helps remove suspended particles, dirt and other physical impurities.

03

03 Activated Carbon

Activated carbon filtration helps reduce unwanted odour, colour and organic impurities, improving the quality of the water.

04

04 Micron Filtration

Fine filtration removes smaller suspended particles from the water before advanced purification.

05

05 Reverse Osmosis

The water passes through an RO membrane to reduce dissolved impurities and unwanted substances.

06

06 Fine Micron Filter

A further micron filtration stage provides additional fine filtration before the final treatment process.

07

07 Ozone Treatment

Ozone treatment provides an additional level of protection by helping control microorganisms in the treated water.

08

08 UV Treatment

UV treatment provides an additional microbial control step before the water moves to quality testing.

09

09 Quality Testing

Water samples are tested in the laboratory for relevant quality parameters to ensure they meet the required standards.

10

10 Hygienic Packaging

After completing the purification and quality checks, the water is hygienically filled, sealed and prepared for distribution.
